Ibn Sina

Ibn Sina**, also known as **Avicenna**, was a famous Muslim physician, philosopher, and scientist. He was born in 980 in the region of Bukhara, in present-day Uzbekistan.

He wrote The Canon of Medicine, one of the most influential medical books in history. This book organized medical knowledge about diseases, treatments, medicines, and the human body.

It was used as a medical textbook in parts of Europe and the Islamic world for centuries. Ibn Sina also made important contributions to philosophy, astronomy, and natural sciences.
