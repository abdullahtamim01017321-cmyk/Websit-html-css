Al-Biruni

Abu Rayhan al-Biruni** was a great Muslim mathematician, astronomer, geographer, and historian. He was born in 973 in Khwarazm, in present-day Uzbekistan.

He made important contributions to astronomy, mathematics, geography, and the study of cultures. He studied the Earth’s measurements and used mathematical methods to estimate its size.

He also wrote about India, its culture, religion, and scientific knowledge.
