Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi

Al-Khwarizmi** was a famous Muslim mathematician, astronomer, and scholar. He lived during the 9th century and worked in Baghdad.

He is known as one of the founders of algebra. His book The Compendious Book on Calculation by Completion and Balancing helped develop systematic methods for solving equations.

The word algebra comes from the Arabic word al-jabr, and the word algorithm comes from the Latin form of his name.

His work had a major influence on mathematics and computer science.

Main contribution: Algebra and mathematical algorithms.
